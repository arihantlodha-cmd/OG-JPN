# Raising the consumption tax

Japan's consumption tax rose from 8 to 10 percent in October 2019, and the IMF has recommended raising it gradually to 15 percent. This reform raises it from 10 to 12 and to 15 percent.

Each reform is solved two ways on Japan's demographics: as a comparison of long-run steady states, and on the full transition path, which shows how the economy moves year by year and gives the net present value of the change. Regenerate them with `examples/analysis_consumption_tax.py` and `examples/analysis_consumption_tax_tpi.py`.

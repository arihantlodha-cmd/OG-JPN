# Data sources and references

## Data

* **Cabinet Office, Economic and Social Research Institute.** *2023年度国民経済計算年次推計* (Annual National Accounts for FY2023): stock edition summary (household balance sheet, table 5) and flow edition summary (nominal GDP). https://www.esri.cao.go.jp/jp/sna/data/data_list/kakuhou/files/2023/2023_kaku_top.html
* **FRED / Penn World Table.** Share of labor compensation in GDP for Japan (`LABSHPJPA156NRUG`); capital stock (`RKNANPJPA666NRUG`) and real GDP (`RGDPNAJPA666NRUG`). https://fred.stlouisfed.org/
* **International Monetary Fund.** *World Economic Outlook*, via the DataMapper API: gross general government debt (`GGXWDG_NGDP`), revenue, expenditure, net lending (`GGXCNL_NGDP`), real GDP growth (`NGDP_RPCH`).
* **Japan Pension Service; Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare.** Social insurance contribution rates.
* **National Tax Agency.** Consumption tax rate; *民間給与実態統計調査* (Statistical Survey of Actual Status for Salary in the Private Sector), 2023 and 2024. https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/statistics/kokuzeicho/minkan/top.htm
* **OECD.** *Taxing Wages 2025*.
* **PwC.** *Worldwide Tax Summaries: Japan* (individual and corporate taxes). JETRO, corporate tax rates.
* **Statistics Bureau of Japan.** *2019年全国家計構造調査 結果の概要* (2019 National Survey of Family Income, Consumption and Wealth, summary of results), 18 May 2021. https://www.stat.go.jp/data/zenkokukakei/2019/pdf/gaiyou0518.pdf
* **United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division.** *World Population Prospects*, via the UN Data Portal and the EAPD-DRB/Population-Data mirror.

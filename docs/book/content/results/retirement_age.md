# Raising the pension eligibility age

Japan's public pension starts at 65. This reform raises the eligibility age to 68 and to 70, as several aging economies have done or are debating.

Each reform is solved two ways on Japan's demographics: as a comparison of long-run steady states, and on the full transition path, which shows how the economy moves year by year and gives the net present value of the change. Regenerate them with `examples/analysis_retirement_age.py` and `examples/analysis_retirement_age_tpi.py`.
